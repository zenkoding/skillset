
## Codex

### Delegation

Delegate bounded, independent work when it improves quality, keeps useful lead
context, or cuts total effort. Keep trivial tasks and tightly coupled reasoning
with the lead. Pick the worker by ambiguity and how the result gets verified:

- GPT-6 Luna: targeted search at medium effort; well-specified implementation
  at high, or max for harder bounded work with objective checks.
- GPT-6.1 Sol: open-ended investigation and substantial implementation at
  high; independent correctness review at xhigh.
- GPT-6 Astra: hard cross-cutting decisions, deep root-cause analysis and
  synthesis. Low is the lead's default; high for hard problems.

Set model and effort explicitly when the tool supports it. Give workers the
minimum context, a clear deliverable, acceptance criteria and disjoint edits.
Review what comes back; don't poll for status or do the work twice.
